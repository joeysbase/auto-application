# 2026-10-05 — `auto-app ui`: Jobs tabs, the Apply section, a short key bar

## What
At your request, before designing the browser-agent applier:

- **Jobs (F1) tabs** are now All · Not evaluated · **New recommended** · **All recommended** · Un-recommended · Applied · Not applied.
  - **New recommended** holds recommended jobs you haven't decided on. The app opens here.
  - **All recommended** holds every job a filter recommended, whatever you decided.
  - **Want to apply** is gone: it duplicated Apply → Queued.
  - **Archived** is gone from the app (`job list` / `filter audit` still show archived jobs).
- **A decision moves the job out at once.** `a` (want to apply) takes it out of New recommended into **Apply → Queued**, and `n` into Not applied. Before, a decided job stayed in its tab until you quit, marked with an arrow.
  - `u` undoes.
  - `r` right after `a` / `n` adds a reason to that decision, even though the job has left the list.
- **The Inbox section is now called Apply (F3)**: it's Step 3. The section bar shows e.g. "Apply (5 queued, 7 ready)", and the status messages and help say Apply. The `apply inbox` command keeps its name.
- **The bottom line is short:** a key bar of our own replaces Textual's footer.
  - It shows only the **main keys of the current tab**, then `? all keys · q quit`, and each is clickable.
  - Jobs: `a want to apply · n not apply · g should recommend · b shouldn't · / search`.
  - Apply → Ready to submit: `s submit · f fill only · e edit`. The other tabs have their own few keys.
  - Every other key still works and is listed by `?`, which now also mentions `ctrl+p`. The "^p palette" entry is gone from the bottom; the palette itself still works.
- The tab bars drop the " · " separators so seven Jobs tabs fit in 140 columns.
